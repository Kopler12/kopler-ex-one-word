## The word

# Forgettable

That's it. That's the whole entry — one word, eleven letters, zero
defence.

## Why this is the word

Ask an ex to describe you in one word and you pray for something
dramatic. *Toxic.* *Crazy.* *Unforgettable.* Anything with a story
behind it, anything that proves you mattered enough to leave a mark.

"Forgettable" is worse than all of them. It doesn't mean the
relationship was bad — it means it barely registered. Not the villain
of the story, not even the tragic hero. Just a background extra who
got written out between episodes and nobody in the audience noticed.

It's the most painfully honest verdict an ex can hand you: you weren't
hated, you weren't missed, you were simply... not remembered. Every
other insult at least admits you existed. This one quietly files you
under "oh right, that guy."

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes exactly `Forgettable` — one word, no trailing newline,
11 bytes — to `/output/entry.txt`.
